In this project, I am investigating the quantum measurement theory of observables in holography, a framework for quantum gravity which posits that a theory of spacetime can be modeled exactly in a corresponding quantum theory without gravity. For example, we are developing the theory for how to measure the length of a wormhole in experimental quantum simulation platforms. Stay tuned for a result!
